UV Index Support

If you have questions or need help with the app, please contact us:

  • Email: dimadyuk.dev@gmail.com
  • Common issues:
    • How to restore purchases and subscriptions (Settings → Restore purchases)
    • The app shows the wrong location or no UV data: allow location access in your device settings, or search for and add your city
    • How to set your skin type and sunscreen (SPF) and start the SPF timer
    • How to turn UV alerts on or off and choose their time
    • How to add the home-screen widget
    • How to set up the Apple Watch or Wear OS app and its complications
    • How to manage or cancel a subscription (Google Play / App Store)

When you write, please include your device model, operating system version and app version (Settings → About) so we can help faster.

We aim to respond within 24–48 hours.
