Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the UnitConverter app for Android and iOS (App ID: com.dimadyuk.unitconverter), together with any related services operated by Dzmitry Dzemidziuk (collectively, the "Application"). Dzmitry Dzemidziuk is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider". This single policy covers both platforms; where a rule applies to only one of them, the platform is stated explicitly.


Summary

The Application converts units of measurement entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, requires no registration, and the conversions themselves work offline. The Service Provider operates no servers of its own and never receives the values you convert, your conversion history, your favourites, or your settings. The Application displays no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What does leave your device is limited to: the information exchanged with the app store and the subscription-management provider when you make or restore a purchase, and the usage and crash diagnostics collected by Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics so that the Service Provider can understand which features are used and fix crashes. All of this is described below, and analytics and crash reporting can be switched off in the Application's settings.


Data Stored On Your Device Only

The following is created by you, stays on your device, and is never transmitted to the Service Provider or to any third party:

  • Conversion input and results — the numbers and units you enter are processed on your device only.
  • App settings (theme, number format) — stored locally using on-device preference storage.
  • Favourites (selected categories and units) — stored locally using on-device preference storage.
  • Conversion history, where the feature is available — stored locally in an on-device database.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

The Application uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to understand how the Application is used in aggregate and to detect and fix defects. These services are used for product improvement only — never for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

  • Firebase Analytics may process: a randomly generated app-instance identifier; the events you trigger inside the Application, such as opening a screen, starting a conversion, or completing a purchase; the app version; the device model, operating system and version; the device language; the coarse country or region derived from your IP address; and session timing. The parameters of your conversions — the numbers you enter, the units you pick, and the results — are not sent as event data.
  • Firebase Crashlytics may process, when the Application crashes or reports a non-fatal error: the stack trace and exception details; a Crashlytics installation identifier; the app version and build; the device model, operating system and version, orientation, and available memory and storage; the time of the crash; and the technical breadcrumb log leading up to it. It does not capture the contents of your screen or your conversion data.
  • Neither service receives your name, email address, phone number, contacts, files, or precise location.

How to opt out. You can disable analytics and crash reporting at any time in the Application's settings; when disabled, no further analytics events or crash reports are sent. Where the law of your country requires your prior consent for this processing, the Application asks for it before any analytics or crash data is collected, and no such data is collected unless you agree. You may also reset the app-instance identifier by reinstalling the Application, and Android users may additionally use the system-level "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" setting.

Google processes this data as the Service Provider's processor under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. Google's own handling of the data is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.


Data Processed By Third Parties

The Application uses the following third-party services. They act as independent controllers or as processors acting on the Service Provider's instructions, and they process the data described here in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics — as described in the section above.
  • RevenueCat (subscription and purchase management) — when you make, restore, or check a purchase, RevenueCat may process an anonymous App User ID generated by the Application, device and operating system information, purchase and transaction information, and the receipt or purchase token issued by Apple or Google. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email address from the Application, and the Service Provider never receives your payment card details.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit (iOS) and Google Play / Google Play Billing (Android) — process the purchase itself, including payment, subscription status, and receipts, under your store account. The Service Provider receives only aggregate, non-identifying sales reporting from the stores.
  • Google Play In-App Review (Android) and SKStoreReviewController (iOS) — if the Application invites you to rate it, the review is submitted to and handled by the store. The Service Provider does not see whether you were asked, whether you responded, or what you wrote, other than as a public store review.

As part of delivering these services, the providers and their infrastructure may observe technical information such as your IP address and device identifiers. This is inherent to any network request and is described in their own privacy policies, linked below.


What The Application Does Not Collect

The Application does not collect, and the Service Provider does not receive:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, files, or microphone or camera data
  • Your precise or coarse device location — only the country or region inferred by Google from your IP address for analytics purposes
  • The values you convert, your conversion history, your favourites, or your settings
  • Advertising identifiers — see the next section

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application does not use cookies. It contains no advertising and displays no ads.

The Application does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Analytics and crash data are used solely to improve the Application and are not combined with data from other sources to build a profile of you or to target advertising. Specifically:

  • Android — the Application does not use the Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID) for advertising or cross-app tracking purposes. Firebase Analytics identifies an installation by a randomly generated app-instance identifier, not by an advertising identifier. In accordance with the Google Play Data Safety declaration for this Application, user data is not used to track users.
  • iOS — the Application does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). No App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown because the Application does not engage in cross-app tracking as defined by Apple's App Store guidelines.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, the processing described above rests on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for analytics and crash reporting, where your prior consent is required for the storing of and access to information on your device. You may withdraw your consent at any time in the Application's settings, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — processing necessary to deliver the Application's paid features, to verify your entitlement, and to restore your purchase across your devices.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — the Service Provider's interest in keeping the Application stable and secure and in understanding, in aggregate, which features are used, where that interest is not overridden by your rights and freedoms and to the extent consent is not required.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law, including tax and consumer-protection obligations relating to purchases.

Data Retention
  • Data on your device (settings, favourites, conversion history) — retained until you delete it in the Application or uninstall the Application. The Service Provider has no access to it and therefore cannot delete it for you.
  • Analytics data — user-level and event-level analytics data are retained in Firebase for the retention period configured for this Application, after which they are deleted automatically; aggregated, non-identifying reporting may be retained indefinitely.
  • Crash reports — retained in Crashlytics for the period set by the service, typically no more than 90 days for an individual crash record.
  • Purchase records — retained by RevenueCat, Apple, and Google according to each provider's own retention policy and applicable tax law; see the privacy policies linked above.
  • Correspondence — if you email the Service Provider, that message and its address are retained for as long as needed to answer you and to keep a record of the request, and no longer than 24 months thereafter unless a longer period is required by law.

Security

All network communication of the Application takes place over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Data stored on your device is protected by the security mechanisms of your operating system's application sandbox. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.


Device Backups

Uninstalling the Application normally removes the settings, favourites, and conversion history stored locally on the device, subject to the backup and restore mechanisms of your operating system: where device backup is enabled, a copy of this data may exist in your own Google or Apple backup and may be restored when you reinstall the Application or set up a new device. Those backups are held under your own store account, not by the Service Provider.
