Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the UnitConverter app for Android and iOS (App ID: com.dimadyuk.unitconverter), together with any related services operated by Dzmitry Dzemidziuk (collectively, the "Application"). Dzmitry Dzemidziuk is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider". This single policy covers both platforms; where a rule applies to only one of them, the platform is stated explicitly.


Summary

The Application converts units of measurement entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, requires no registration, and works offline. The Service Provider operates no servers, collects no personal data, and receives no information about you or about the values you convert. The Application contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising.

The only data that leaves your device is the information exchanged with the app store and the subscription-management provider when you make or restore a purchase, as described below.


Data Stored On Your Device Only

The following is created by you, stays on your device, and is never transmitted to the Service Provider or to any third party:

  • Conversion input and results — the numbers and units you enter are processed on your device only.
  • App settings (theme, number format) — stored locally using on-device preference storage.
  • Favourites (selected categories and units) — stored locally using on-device preference storage.
  • Conversion history, where the feature is available — stored locally in an on-device database.

Data Processed By Third Parties

The Application uses the following third-party services. They act as independent controllers or as processors acting on the Service Provider's instructions, and they process the data described here in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • RevenueCat (subscription and purchase management) — when you make, restore, or check a purchase, RevenueCat may process an anonymous App User ID generated by the Application, device and operating system information, purchase and transaction information, and the receipt or purchase token issued by Apple or Google. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email address from the Application, and the Service Provider never receives your payment card details.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit (iOS) and Google Play / Google Play Billing (Android) — process the purchase itself, including payment, subscription status, and receipts, under your store account. The Service Provider receives only aggregate, non-identifying sales reporting from the stores.
  • Google Play In-App Review (Android) and SKStoreReviewController (iOS) — if the Application invites you to rate it, the review is submitted to and handled by the store. The Service Provider does not see whether you were asked, whether you responded, or what you wrote, other than as a public store review.

As part of delivering these services, the providers and their infrastructure may observe technical information such as your IP address and device identifiers. This is inherent to any network request and is described in their own privacy policies, linked below.


What The Application Does Not Collect

The Application does not collect, and the Service Provider does not receive:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, files, or microphone or camera data
  • Your precise or coarse location
  • Analytics events or usage statistics — the Application contains no analytics SDK
  • Crash reports or diagnostic logs — the Application contains no crash-reporting SDK
  • The values you convert, your conversion history, your favourites, or your settings
  • Advertising identifiers — see the next section

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application does not use cookies. It contains no advertising and displays no ads.

The Application does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Specifically:

  • Android — the Application does not use the Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID) for advertising or cross-app tracking purposes. In accordance with the Google Play Data Safety declaration for this Application, user data is not used to track users.
  • iOS — the Application does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). No App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown because the Application does not engage in cross-app tracking as defined by Apple's App Store guidelines.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the limited processing described above rests on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to deliver the Application's paid features, to verify your entitlement, and to restore your purchase across your devices.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law, including tax and consumer-protection obligations relating to purchases.

The Service Provider does not rely on legitimate interests for analytics or crash reporting, because the Application performs neither.


Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — to obtain a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification — to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — to request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych).

Because the Service Provider holds no personal data about you, a request addressed to the Service Provider will usually be answered by confirming that no such data exists, and by directing you to the relevant third-party provider or app store where their records are concerned. Data held on your device is under your own control and can be deleted in the Application or by uninstalling it. To exercise these rights, contact the Service Provider at dimadyuk.dev@gmail.com.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. The Service Provider does not sell or share your personal information, and has not done so in the preceding twelve months. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact the Service Provider at dimadyuk.dev@gmail.com.


Third-Party Privacy Policies

The third-party services used by the Application have their own privacy policies:

  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit (iOS)
  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing (Android)

International Data Transfers

RevenueCat, Apple, and Google may process data in countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Those transfers are governed by the transfer mechanisms described in each provider's privacy policy, typically Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission or an adequacy decision. The Service Provider itself transfers no personal data, because it holds none.


Data Retention
  • Data on your device (settings, favourites, conversion history) — retained until you delete it in the Application or uninstall the Application. The Service Provider has no access to it and therefore cannot delete it for you.
  • Data held by the Service Provider — none. The Service Provider operates no servers and maintains no user database.
  • Data held by third-party providers — retained according to each provider's own retention policy; see the privacy policies linked above. RevenueCat retains purchase and entitlement records for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet its own legal obligations; Apple and Google retain purchase records under their respective policies and applicable tax law.
  • Correspondence — if you email the Service Provider, that message and its address are retained for as long as needed to answer you and to keep a record of the request, and no longer than 24 months thereafter unless a longer period is required by law.

Children

The Application is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided the Service Provider with personal information, please contact dimadyuk.dev@gmail.com so that the necessary actions can be taken.


Security

Data created by you inside the Application never leaves your device, which by design limits its exposure. On the device it is protected by the operating system's own application sandbox and, where enabled by you, by device encryption. The Service Provider provides appropriate technical and organisational safeguards for the limited information it handles, such as email correspondence.


Device Backups

Uninstalling the Application normally removes the settings, favourites, and conversion history stored locally on the device, subject to the backup and restore mechanisms of your operating system: where device backup is enabled, a copy of this data may exist in your own Google or Apple backup and may be restored when you reinstall the Application or set up a new device. Those backups are held under your own store account, not by the Service Provider.

If a data breach occurs that affects your personal data, the Service Provider will notify you and the competent supervisory authority in accordance with applicable legal requirements, including, where required, providing information about the nature of the breach and the steps being taken to address it.
